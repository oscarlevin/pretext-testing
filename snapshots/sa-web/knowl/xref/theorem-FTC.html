Theorem 2.1. The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus.

If \(f(x)\) is continuous, and the derivative of \(F(x)\) is \(f(x)\text{,}\) then
\begin{equation*} \definiteintegral{a}{b}{f(x)}{x}=F(b)-F(a) \end{equation*}
